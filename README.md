**Start here: [one compound at 90°, explained intuitively and mathematically](docs/55_zenith_single_compound_walkthrough.md).** The October 5 revision implements a contiguous 10 GHz example, explicit matrices and observation time, and [responds to every supervisor note with sources](docs/54_supervisor_revision_2026_10_05.md). With 25 dBm total power, 10 cm / 1 m apertures, 20 s reference plus sample and an **assumed, unmeasured 0.001 dB calibration residual**, the single compound pilot detector has a predicted 95% acetonitrile enhancement limit of **50.58 µg/m³**; simulated response is **94.75%** there and only **1.31% at 1 µg/m³**. This is a different benchmark from the older payload studies below. Background atmospheric sensitivity is not demonstrated.

**LEO links are a useful running example for every part of the study:** geometry, atmospheric propagation, RF hardware, antennas, OFDM, Doppler, observation time, chemical detection and future sensing with multiple ground receivers. The methods can be adapted to terrestrial and UAV links with their own geometry and hardware.

> **CRITICAL: The original favorable broad reference results assume zero residual calibration error after correction. This accuracy has not been demonstrated experimentally.** At 45° in the standard atmosphere, with 10 s reference + 10 s sensing, predicted acetonitrile recall at 1 µg/m³ falls from **93.84% to 25.17%** when the modeled differential residual standard deviation rises from 0 to 0.001 dB. Receiver noise and finite reference uncertainty remain present in both cases. [Permanent calibration note, exact results and claim conditions](docs/46_critical_calibration_assumption.md).

**Latest five-gas design:** At 20 s total and an **assumed, unmeasured 0.0001 dB residual**, acetonitrile recall at 1 µg/m³ improves to **97.67%** with all five VOCs and fine/coarse PM jointly fitted. Results now include **2/20/100 s × 0/0.0001/0.001 dB**, low recall percentages and conditional 95% limits for every target. Useful PM remains unsupported; the architecture also has a communication tradeoff. [Full tables](docs/51_joint_design_time_calibration_results.md), [every known remaining issue](docs/49_completion_audit_and_fixes.md), [species and methods](docs/50_species_methods_and_percentage_guide.md), [current manifest](results/joint_receiver_revision/deliverable_manifest.json).

**Receiver extension with nonzero calibration:** A sequential 16 MHz OFDM design now has exact spectroscopy, moving raw coded receiver controls, charged retuning and calibration acceptance budgets. At 100 s total, acetonitrile recall at 1 µg/m³ is **99.99% with an assumed 0.0001 dB residual**, but only **3.14% at 0.001 dB**. Neither accuracy is measured. Retuning costs 1.382% of scheduled payload versus a fixed band, and required RF power/gain stability remain unproven. [Full findings and remaining gaps](docs/47_receiver_design_and_calibration.md), [new evidence manifest](results/receiver_design/deliverable_manifest.json).

**Requested 20 s, 0.0001 dB control:** With three VOCs plus fine/coarse PM, simulated acetonitrile recall at 1 µg/m³ is **90.20%**. Adding HITRAN chloromethane and formic acid to the joint fit reduces it to **56.63%**. PM mass detection remains ineffective, and splitting the fine mode at 1 µm is rejected as numerically unstable. The residual calibration is assumed, not measured. [Expanded VOC and PM results](docs/48_expanded_voc_pm_and_20s_calibration.md).

**Payload information and proposal Tasks 4.1–4.3:** The finite reference analysis now includes full QPSK and magnitude Fisher bounds, recomputed elevation/weather sensitivity, moving geometry information and validated detection floors in µg/m³ and ppm. M2M4 retains about **96–98%** of the full QPSK information at the 45° reference, so estimator replacement alone offers little improvement there. [Results and limitations](docs/45_payload_bounds_results.md), [derivation](docs/44_payload_information_derivation.md), [remaining proposal tasks](docs/43_original_proposal_remaining_tasks.md).

**September 27 recall improvement:** Passive QPSK payload sensing increases simulated 1 µg/m³ acetonitrile recall from **0.460% to 99.955% at 10 s**, using the same transmission resources and false alarm threshold. Formaldehyde reaches **95.275% at 100 s**. A finite reference comparison gives **93.800%** acetonitrile enhancement recall with 10 s reference plus 10 s sensing. Calibration, weather and the practical E-band limitation remain decisive. [Derivation, Google paper analysis, full results and reproduction](docs/42_payload_recall.md).

**The public data support conditional computational evidence; a calibrated atmospheric VOC/PM sensor and environmental compliance are not demonstrated.** Earlier work adds environmental domain/averaging checks, actual OFDM decoding, moving-pass and synchronization controls, PM information diagnosis, a published 380 GHz water comparison and measured public calcite transmission. Calcite blanks drift by up to 0.090 dB and lack mass/size labels, so these measurements do not calibrate PM concentration.
