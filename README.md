# THz sensing

> **CRITICAL: Favorable recall and detection limits assume zero residual calibration error after correction. This calibration accuracy has not been demonstrated experimentally.** At 45° in the standard atmosphere, with 10 s reference + 10 s sensing, predicted acetonitrile recall at 1 µg/m³ falls from **93.84% to 25.17%** when the modeled differential residual standard deviation rises from 0 to 0.001 dB. Receiver noise and finite reference uncertainty remain present in both cases. [Permanent calibration note, exact results and claim conditions](docs/46_critical_calibration_assumption.md).

**Payload information and proposal Tasks 4.1–4.3:** The finite reference analysis now includes full QPSK and magnitude Fisher bounds, recomputed elevation/weather sensitivity, moving geometry information and validated detection floors in µg/m³ and ppm. M2M4 retains about **96–98%** of the full QPSK information at the 45° reference, so estimator replacement alone offers little improvement there. [Results and limitations](docs/45_payload_bounds_results.md), [derivation](docs/44_payload_information_derivation.md), [remaining proposal tasks](docs/43_original_proposal_remaining_tasks.md).

**September 27 recall improvement:** Passive QPSK payload sensing increases simulated 1 µg/m³ acetonitrile recall from **0.460% to 99.955% at 10 s**, using the same transmission resources and false alarm threshold. Formaldehyde reaches **95.275% at 100 s**. A finite reference comparison gives **93.800%** acetonitrile enhancement recall with 10 s reference plus 10 s sensing. Calibration, weather and the practical E-band limitation remain decisive. [Derivation, Google paper analysis, full results and reproduction](docs/42_payload_recall.md).

The [current manuscript](output/pdf/thz_isac_pollutant_sensing_ieee.pdf) includes both payload extensions. Its [current deliverable manifest](results/payload_bounds/report/deliverable_manifest.json) records the updated files. The September 23 [five-task results report](docs/40_five_task_closure.md) explains the preceding assessment, maps every task in the original proposal, links evidence and gives reproduction commands.

**The public data support conditional computational evidence; a calibrated atmospheric VOC/PM sensor and environmental compliance are not demonstrated.** Earlier work adds environmental domain/averaging checks, actual OFDM decoding, moving-pass and synchronization controls, PM information diagnosis, a published 380 GHz water comparison and measured public calcite transmission. The practical E-band reference preserves communication under read-only pilot reuse but fails useful joint sensing. Calcite blanks drift by up to 0.090 dB and lack mass/size labels, so these measurements do not calibrate PM concentration.
