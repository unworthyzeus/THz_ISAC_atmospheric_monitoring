\subsection{Additional VOCs and PM size fractions}
The requested 20 s, 0.0001 dB case was extended using newly acquired HITRAN CH$_3$Cl and HCOOH lines, with two available isotopologues each. Four other organic candidates remain acquisition gaps. Jointly fitting five VOCs and two PM masses, with the same background/gain nuisance terms and a 1\% family budget over eight outputs, reduces simulated 1 \ugm\ acetonitrile recall to 56.63\% in 10,000 joint-mixture responses. Family false alarms are 0.68\%. Conditional 95\% response limits are 12.114 \ugm\ for CH$_3$Cl and 200.879 \ugm\ for HCOOH, validated at 95.11\% and 95.16\% simulated recall. Additional species therefore expose ambiguity in the frozen receiver schedule.

PM2.5/PM10 recall at 49/90 \ugm\ remains only 0.08\%, at the false-alarm scale. Even with all other quantities known, a one-parameter fine-PM limit is approximately 905 \ugm. Subdividing the fine mode into aerodynamic cuts below and above 1 $\mu$m produces a numerically unstable three-bin inverse problem: its scaled identity error is 0.508 and the result is rejected. Composition remains assumed; missing particle mass/size calibration cannot be replaced by additional simulated material labels. These results use the same assumed 0.0001 dB residual, matched reference weather and unverified RF requirements. They are not measured field performance.
