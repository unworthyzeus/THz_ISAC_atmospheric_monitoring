# Complete audit of remaining work and this revision's fixes

September 27, 2026. Checked directly against the two-page [original proposal](../I2R_proposal_THz_ISAC%20(1).pdf), the source code, retained numerical evidence, the current manuscript and the receiver/VOC/PM extensions. This is the current inventory of known gaps; historical result reports describe their own snapshots.

**The eleven requested modeling/simulation tasks have implementations. The intended successful joint VOC/PM sensor, demonstrated calibration accuracy and environmental compliance do not.** A negative feasibility result completes an assessment but does not satisfy a positive sensing-performance ambition. This revision improves the frozen five-VOC design, adds explicit time/calibration percentage tables, explains the targets/methods and updates the completion interpretation.

Read the [results with exact operating conditions](51_joint_design_time_calibration_results.md) and [species/method/percentage guide](50_species_methods_and_percentage_guide.md). Favorable values always require the stated calibration assumption. Zero additional residual remains an ideal benchmark; nonzero residual values are also unmeasured assumptions.

## The original eleven tasks

| Task | Implemented evidence | Current boundary |
|---|---|---|
| 1.1 Stratified atmosphere | Standard atmosphere, public weather soundings, temperature/pressure/water profiles. | Pollutant vertical distribution and atmospheric horizontal variability are not measured. |
| 1.2 Spectroscopy extraction | Traceable HITRAN Voigt inputs, isotope handling, five target gases and four interfering gases. | Four additional organic candidates lack suitable lines in the published HITRAN window; spectroscopy uncertainty and missing minor isotopes remain. |
| 1.3 Slant-path absorption | Refracted spherical paths, temperature-dependent line strengths and broadening, numerical checks. | Forward physics needs independent experimental validation and broader model-error characterization. |
| 1.4 Particle baseline | Rayleigh and full Mie mass-normalized size distributions. | Useful mass/size/composition retrieval is not demonstrated. |
| 2.1 Link parameterization | Power/noise/antenna models, sequential one-chain OFDM bands, bandwidth, CP, pilots, reference and settling charged. | Required RF hardware performance and lawful spectrum use are not established. |
| 2.2 Simulated CSI | AWGN, complex pilots, raw IFFT/CP frames, bounded moving Doppler acquisition and coded payload checks. | Full time dilation, fractional timing, clock/oscillator/pointing errors and realistic reference scheduling remain open. |
| 3.1 Spectral isolation | Joint gas/PM GLS, nuisance projection, rank and scaled numerical identity gates; now a design selected for all five VOCs. | Arbitrary gain drift and nearly collinear PM signatures remain failure mechanisms. |
| 3.2 Inversion | Linear/statistical and nonlinear response calculations, signed errors, detection trials and retained failures. | No useful general PM inversion or universal low-concentration VOC success. Absolute reference abundance is unknown. |
| 4.1 Variance bounds | QPSK/magnitude Fisher bounds, unknown noise/reference handling, GLS covariance, moment efficiency checks. | These are local conditional estimation bounds, not universal detection or hardware guarantees. |
| 4.2 Sensitivity | Elevation, weather, power/noise, time, calibration and nuisance sensitivity; this revision adds five-gas design comparisons. | Limited weather profiles do not establish population robustness or unknown-weather correction. |
| 4.3 Detection floors and standards | Mass/ppm limits, positive-response variance, binomial recall intervals, full time/calibration grid and explicit failed PM floors. | A short slant enhancement is not a standards-compliant ambient concentration measurement. No positive compliance result exists. |

Curve fitting, ratiometric inference and machine learning are alternatives in Task 3.2; adding a neural network is not an unfulfilled mandatory task. Historical Tasks 1.5, 3.3 and 4.4 are optional additions. The abstract's communication-capacity objective is assessed separately below.

## What a complete research deliverable means here

A defensible computational deliverable contains the physical assumptions, implemented models, analytical bounds, independently selected/evaluated designs, readable percentages and uncertainty, resource accounting, failed cases and reproducible evidence. This revision fills the identified reporting and design omissions in that deliverable.

A **validated useful atmospheric VOC/PM instrument** requires more: achievable RF/calibration specifications, informative PM observables, realistic dynamics/reference conditions and independent paired measurements. These are substantial open milestones. A simulation cannot supply missing measurements or prove favorable field performance by construction.

The highest-priority next physical input is a repeated blank/reference campaign at the selected tones, with independent concentration standards and PM mass/size characterization. Before hardware access, the most useful further computational work is full warped-time receiver simulation and a design study for an additional PM observable. Both should be judged by the same fixed-resource and independent-evaluation criteria, not by optimizer convergence alone.
