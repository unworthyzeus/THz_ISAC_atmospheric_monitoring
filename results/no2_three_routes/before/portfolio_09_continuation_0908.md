## Priority result: THz

**RESULT / SELF-DERIVED:** With an assumed persistent per-probe error radius of ±0.0001 dB, the exact rational dual lower bound for NO₂'s noise-free normalized bias is 1.9837335 in the represented 202-probe LP. This exceeds the unit target independently of pilot resources, within the declared unbiased linear class and finite atmospheric hull plus spectral-error box. The certificate does not enclose uncertain physical coefficients, validate the envelope empirically or cover nonlinear/prior-assisted estimators. [All 24 bounds](../../important_research_because_its_for_n3cat/THz_ISAC_atmospheric_monitoring/results/continuation_calibration_floor/floors.csv), [independent exact dual checks](../results/continuation_0908/verification/exact_dual_checks.json).

**RESULT:** At ten million ideal simultaneous pilots, calibration-aware NO₂ stress-replay RMSE improves from 3.385 to 2.425 at the same radius, but fails the target. At 30,000 pilots CO and SO₂ retain worst replay errors .290 and .809. The 64 atmospheric states are reused stress data, not a fresh independent sample. [Complete 512-row result table](../../important_research_because_its_for_n3cat/THz_ISAC_atmospheric_monitoring/results/continuation_calibration/summary.csv).

**RESULT / INFERENCE:** The 71-tone sequential 260–400 GHz scenario explicitly charges dwell, source power and energy. At −1 dBm per tone and ten seconds, it gives 140,845 pilots per tone, .0079433 J radiated energy and NO₂ stress-replay error 7.654. The same LEO propagation and coherent receiver likelihood remain assumed; a typical extender specification is not a demonstrated satellite instrument. [Resources](../../important_research_because_its_for_n3cat/THz_ISAC_atmospheric_monitoring/results/continuation_calibration/resources.csv), [risk table](../../important_research_because_its_for_n3cat/THz_ISAC_atmospheric_monitoring/results/continuation_calibration/summary.csv), [source record](../../important_research_because_its_for_n3cat/THz_ISAC_atmospheric_monitoring/results/continuation_calibration/source_record.json).

No paired attenuation/truth field dataset was acquired. The [bounded source search](../../important_research_because_its_for_n3cat/THz_ISAC_atmospheric_monitoring/docs/continuation_source_search.md) records that limitation; the [concrete measurement protocol](../../important_research_because_its_for_n3cat/THz_ISAC_atmospheric_monitoring/docs/continuation_measurement_protocol.md) specifies the next external evidence required.

- **Online EM, INFERENCE:** Test misspecified reporting errors and additional declared drift laws on new streams; field performance requires measured sensor data. [Scope and justification](../../online_em_time_varying_ddwsn/docs/continuation_0908.md).
- **Beamforming, INFERENCE:** Retain the power-budget guarantee and qualify latency by fallback incidence; evaluate deployment tails on a separately specified workload. [Scope and justification](../../multiuser_short_packet_beamforming/docs/continuation_0908.md).
- **PIM, INFERENCE:** Preserve the common-alphabet comparison and evaluate a separately specified channel family before broader design claims. [Scope and justification](../../PIM_joint_partition_energy_design/docs/continuation_0908.md).
- **Flow compression, INFERENCE:** Evaluate full-resolution color images, model-seed variation and meaningful rate/distortion budgets before a general codec claim. [Scope and justification](../../flow_methods_signal_neural_coding/docs/continuation_0908.md).
