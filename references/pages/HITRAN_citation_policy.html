Citation Policy

When using data from HITRAN, please remember to acknowledge the hard work and dedication of the scientific community that makes this resource possible. Your citations matter.
 

   Required Citation

Please cite the article describing the database edition that you are using. The current edition is HITRAN2024 (corresponding to the data here), and can be cited as:

  • I. E. Gordon, L. S. Rothman, R. J. Hargreaves, F. M. Gomez, T. Bertin, C. Hill, et al., "The HITRAN2024 molecular spectroscopic database", J. Quant. Spectrosc. Radiat. Transfer 353, 109807 (2026). [doi:10.1016/j.jqsrt.2026.109807]

The article can be viewed here and the full citation can also be exported to different formats (e.g., BibTeX, EndNote) using the ADS website.
 

   Citing Original Sources

For each parameter in HITRAN, references are also included to the original data sources. We strongly encourage users of HITRAN to cite the original sources when using specific data points. This honors the data contributions from researchers. The video tutorials explain how to retrieve the reference information from the database.
